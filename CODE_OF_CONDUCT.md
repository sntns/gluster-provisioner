# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming, harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and communicate clearly.
- Keep discussions focused on technical topics.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or hateful conduct.
- Publishing private information about others.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please contact the maintainers privately (recommended for sensitive reports): `contact@sentiens.fr`.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, close issues/PRs, or restrict participation to keep the community safe.
